Where Dallas’s draft code mixes homes and shops

16,372 acres, 6.6% of zoned land, become one of four mixed-use (MX) districts under Public Draft 1. The biggest source is today’s retail strips: 8,131 acres of CR and RR become MX-2. Today those districts allow no apartments. MX-2 allows them by right, up to 70 ft, with new buildings at the sidewalk (0–5 ft setback).

Also folded in: the MU mixed-use districts, office districts (NO, LO, MO, GO), MF-3 and MF-4 apartments, and downtown’s CA districts (MX-4). Existing buildings and lots stay until redeveloped. Planned developments are not shown; they keep their own rules.
