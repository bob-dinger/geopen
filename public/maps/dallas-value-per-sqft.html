Downtown, Uptown and the towers along the Tollway carry hundreds or thousands of dollars of value per square foot. A typical house lot carries about $37. Big-box stores, warehouses and their parking carry far less than their size suggests, and half the county's parcel land holds just 2% of its value: floodplain, parks, farmland and industrial yards.
