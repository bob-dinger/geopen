Which Texas counties have a burn ban

—loading

Counties where the commissioners court has adopted an outdoor burning ban. A ban is a legal instrument, not a firebreak — it makes burning an offence, which is a deterrent, not a barrier.

Texas A&M Forest Service burn ban feature service. Counties adopt and lift bans independently, so the map changes daily. geopen.io
