Texas bought this to be its second-largest state park, after Big Bend Ranch, and has published no boundary. This one is assembled from the 115 appraisal parcels still recorded to the Moody Foundation and the Libbie Shearn Moody Trust — the sellers — and comes within 0.4% of the acreage TPWD announced. The paler block adjoins it but was not part of the sale.
