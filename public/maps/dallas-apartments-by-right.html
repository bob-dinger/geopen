Where Dallas allows apartments today

Apartments can be built without a rezoning on 32,578 acres, 13.1% of Dallas’s zoned land. Half comes from the city’s own apartment and mixed-use districts. The other half, 16,334 acres of retail, office, commercial-service and light-industrial land, was opened by SB 840, a state law in force since 1 September 2025 that overrides local zoning. Orange outlines are the 48 Opportunity Zone tracts Texas nominated for 2027–2036.

SB 840 does not apply within 3,000 ft of an airport (grey) or 1,000 ft of an existing heavy industrial site, which no public map shows, so the green is an upper bound. Industrial districts IR and IM are excluded, following the city’s own reading in its draft code. Planned developments (45,022 acres) are not evaluated.
