Where central Dallas streets change their names

Inside the central loop, a street changes its name 88 times without turning: keep driving straight and the sign says something else. 63 of those are on arterials and collectors, among them Turtle Creek into Cedar Springs into Bowen, Maple into Routh, Gaston into Pacific and Young into Canton.

Click a point to light up both streets. A change is counted where one name ends and another begins at an angle within 35° of straight. Short connector segments are followed through, so Maple meets Routh across Woodall Rodgers. Prefix changes (N to S Haskell), alleys, freeways and ramps are not counted. A few points are angled T-junctions where the next street happens to line up.
