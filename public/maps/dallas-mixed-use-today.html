Where Dallas allows homes and shops together today

Dallas’s current code allows apartments and shops on the same site, without a rezoning, on just 6,631 acres, 2.7% of zoned land. Most of it is the MU mixed-use districts and downtown’s CA districts. The draft code’s MX districts would cover 16,372 acres (6.6%), mostly by adding today’s retail strips.

Office and retail districts (NO, LO, MO, GO, CR, RR) are not shown: they allow no apartments, or in GO only 5% of a building. Planned developments are not shown either; many mix uses under their own ordinances.
