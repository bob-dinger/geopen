Two-thirds of Houston runs on citywide rules alone

Houston’s citywide rules cover lot size, setbacks and parking, not use. Anything more comes from the neighborhood: deed restrictions, a petitioned lot size or building line, a historic district. Combine every one the city maps and 67% of Houston, 402.1 sq mi, has none of them.

Deed restrictions are estimated from civic club areas, which is the only proxy available. Industrial and commercial land is part of the orange.
