Harry Hines stations have a black ring and their count shown.

Harry Hines carries 15,000 to 27,000 vehicles a day, against 200,000 to 263,000 on Stemmons a few blocks east. Five of its six annual stations are lower than in 2019. The steepest drop is at the downtown end near Harwood, from 29,500 to 14,600: a single 2025 count, which a lane closure could also explain.

The grey dots are TxDOT's rotating counts, taken at each place once every few years. They add coverage on smaller streets but have no road name in TxDOT's file and no comparable 2019 figure.

TxDOT Transportation Planning and Programming, AADT annual and 5-year count services, November 2026. AADT is annual average daily traffic, both directions.
