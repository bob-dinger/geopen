Vacant land, once the water is out

Dallas County. "Vacant" on the appraisal roll means nothing is built on the land, not that anything could be. This removes what the water rules out, and shows what is left.

Removing the water takes 57% of the acreage but only 16% of the parcels — a few enormous riverside tracts carry most of the land. What remains has a median size of 0.19 acres. A residential lot.

Half of it is inside the city of Dallas: 24,464 parcels, 20,205 acres — about 8% of the city. Then Grand Prairie, Irving, Mesquite, Garland.

The floodway is the channel and the ground that must stay clear to carry a flood; building there is effectively barred. The 1% floodplain is not unbuildable — it requires elevation and flood insurance. Both are removed here, so this is the conservative set.
