The only parts of Houston with city design review

Outside deed restrictions, Houston’s city government reviews what a building looks like in only one kind of place: its 23 city historic districts, 1,538 acres, 0.4% of the city. Independence Heights appears on the city’s map as a proposed conservation district (708 acres).

The conservation district layer carries no ordinance number, so it is shown as proposed.
