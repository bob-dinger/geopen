Who builds and funds DFW

The developers, investors and lenders behind Dallas–Fort Worth real estate: how big each one says it is, how many people it employs, and what it puts money into. Firms describe their size in different ways, and the ways are not interchangeable, so every figure here is labelled by what it measures.

How big each firm says it is

Dollar figures on a log scale, so a $30 million fund and an $80 billion manager fit on one line. Colour says what the number counts. Money managed today and deals done over a firm's whole life are different things; a bank's balance sheet is different again.

Size against headcount

Firms with both a dollar figure and an employee count. Fund managers run billions with a few dozen people; developers and lenders that operate property or branches employ hundreds or thousands. Hollow dots are headcounts known only as a range.

What they invest in

One row per firm, largest first; a filled cell means the firm says it invests in, develops or lends on that kind of property.
