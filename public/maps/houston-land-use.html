What is built on every lot in Houston

Houston has no use zoning: no city rule says where homes, shops or factories may go. Even so, 79% of its 499,437 parcels are single-family homes.

Where the mixing happens, it is at the edges. 4,909 of 13,438 commercial parcels (37%) share a lot line with a house.

Much of the city's housing is governed by private deed restrictions, which the City enforces but does not map. In the 470 civic-club areas, the City's own rough stand-in for them, 4% of parcels are commercial, office or industrial. Outside them the figure is 8%.
