Drag the handle. The left is the zoning in force today. The right is the same land under Public Draft 1 of the new Dallas Development Code, converted with the draft's own table (Table 4-1).

The boundaries do not move. Every district gets a new name and new rules: R-7.5(A), the city's most common district, becomes R-2, where two homes and a backyard unit are allowed on a lot.
