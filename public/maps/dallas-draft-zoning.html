The conversion is mostly a renaming of what is already there: R-7.5(A), the city's most common district at 63,700 acres, becomes R-2. What changes is what the new districts allow. R-1 to R-3 permit a second unit on any lot and small apartment buildings on corner lots with alleys; office and retail districts become mixed-use districts that allow housing.

Planned developments (grey) stay in force. Where a PD ordinance refers to an old district, the same table translates it, and where a PD is silent the new code applies.
