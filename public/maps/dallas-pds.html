Dallas’s planned developments

Dallas has 1,004 planned developments (PDs) covering 45,022 acres, about 18% of the city’s zoned land. Each is its own zoning ordinance, negotiated for one site or neighborhood. The city added 154 in the 1980s, 220 in the 1990s and 230 in the 2000s, and is still adding them: 108 since 2020.

The year is when the PD was first created, from its ordinance’s legislative history; most have been amended since. PD 193 (Oak Lawn) is drawn by subdistrict. Under the draft development code, PDs stay in place as overlays, and where a PD is silent the new code applies.
