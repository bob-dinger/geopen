Where the land is worth more than the building

Downtown and Uptown Dallas parcels where the appraisal district values the building at less than half the land under it. Developers call these soft sites: the land is the asset, which is what makes redeveloping them pay.

Within half a mile of Klyde Warren Park, 221 soft sites hold $634 million of land. Not all of them are parking: Terrace House on Maple Avenue is a 1961 tower of 118 apartments that the roll values at $5.3 million, on $37.8 million of land.

A soft site is not a site for sale. Several of the largest belong to developers who have bought up neighbouring lots and are likely waiting on a project. The deed date in each popup helps tell recent assembly from land held for decades.

DCAD 2026 certified roll. Privately owned parcels of 0.1 acre or more; parks, rail, cemeteries, churches, museums, utilities and public owners are left out. Downtown is the freeway loop; Uptown is everything north of Woodall Rodgers between I-35E and US 75, up to the Uptown PID's northern edge.
