How far DFW home values have fallen from their peak

247 of 250 ZIP codes in Dallas–Fort Worth sit below their own all-time high. Half the metro peaked in July and August 2022.

The pattern is a ring. The exurban edge — Princeton, Anna, Lavon, Aubrey — is down a fifth to a quarter. The urban core barely moved.
