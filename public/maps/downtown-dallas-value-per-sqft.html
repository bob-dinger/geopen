This mile holds 7% of Dallas County's appraised value on under 1% of its land. The peaks are the towers of downtown, Uptown and Victory Park; the low ground in between is surface parking, rail yards, the Trinity levees, and older low-rise blocks. In blue is property that pays no property tax: parks, the Trinity floodway, government buildings, churches, hospitals, the American Airlines Center and the Omni, plus apartments on public title. It is 39% of the land here and 16% of the value. Drag with right-click or two fingers to tilt and turn.

DCAD 2026 certified roll: total market value of land and improvements, divided by each parcel's mapped area. Condo units are summed onto their building; exempt property is included at its appraised value; business equipment and inventory are left out. The inner line is the freeway loop, the outer line one mile beyond it. A few parcels look extreme where DCAD draws a large account on a small piece of land.
