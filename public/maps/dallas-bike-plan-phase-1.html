Phase 1 totals 34.1 miles. The tracker's own grand total reads 61.39, but that counts the 27.26 miles in progress twice.

The plan proposes 543 miles in all. Its 20-year Phase 2 assumes $2.5 million a year from the General Fund; the transfer was $2 million in fiscal 2025-26 and is $1.25 million in 2026-27. The city says Phase 1 stays on schedule.

Of the 88 miles the city layer lists as existing, 51 are sharrow routes: bike symbols painted in a shared travel lane, which the plan calls the least-preferred facility and proposes to upgrade or remove almost everywhere.
